Sample recording page

Take the long way down.

This page gives Smooth Scroll Studio enough room to demonstrate a calm, even recording pass.

01

Begin with stillness

A short delay gives the recording a clean opening frame. Let the page settle before motion begins, especially when fonts, images, or interface elements load asynchronously.

Three seconds is usually enough room to trim the start of a take without feeling rushed.

02

Choose a human pace

Slow scrolling gives the viewer time to read headings and understand the rhythm of a page. The best speed depends on density: visual pages can move faster, while articles need more breathing room.

Adjust the controller during a take if a long quiet section needs a little momentum.

03

Pause on the good parts

Use the pause control whenever an important section enters the frame. The animation resumes from the exact same position and retains the selected speed.

For a clean capture, hide the control panel once the page and speed are set.

04

Finish with room to breathe

The scroll stops automatically at the bottom of the page. Hold the final frame for a moment before ending the recording so the finish feels deliberate.

When you are ready for another take, return to the top and begin again.
